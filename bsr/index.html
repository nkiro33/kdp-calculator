How BSR turns into sales

Best Sellers Rank is Amazon's ranking of every product in a store by recent sales, updated roughly every hour. Amazon doesn't publish sales figures, but rank and sales follow a steady curve: the top ranks sell thousands a day, and sales fall off quickly further down.

This calculator fits that curve through commonly cited reference points for the US Kindle Store: about 1,000 sales a day at #100, 20 a day at #10,000 and 1.2 a day at #100,000. It interpolates on a log scale between them. The Books store uses the same curve × 0.55. Smaller Amazon stores are scaled down, for example amazon.co.uk and amazon.de × 0.3 and amazon.fr × 0.1.

Getting a useful estimate

  • Use the main rank. Take the rank in Books or Kindle Store, not a sub-category like "#3 in Gratitude Journals".
  • Check it on more than one day. A single sale can lift an obscure book 500,000 places for a few hours. Look at the rank on several days, or use a few books, before trusting a niche.
  • Kindle Unlimited borrows count. In the Kindle Store a KU borrow moves the rank like a sale, so a KU-heavy eBook may earn less than its rank suggests.
  • Treat it as a range. Seasonality, launches and ads all shift the curve. Read the number as "about 5 a day", not "exactly 5.2".

Questions

How many books does a BSR of 100,000 sell?

On Amazon.com, a Kindle eBook at #100,000 sells roughly one copy a day. A print book at #100,000 in Books sells roughly one copy every one to two days.

What is a good BSR for a book?

Under about #20,000 means roughly five or more sales a day. Under #100,000 means about a sale a day for a Kindle eBook, or one every other day for a print book. Above #500,000 a book sells only occasionally.

Where do I find a book's BSR?

On the Amazon product page, scroll to Product details and look for "Best Sellers Rank". The first number is the one to use.

How accurate is this?

It's the same kind of estimate KDP research tools use. It's good for comparing books and niches. For a single book, treat it as a ballpark: real sales can easily be half or double the estimate.
